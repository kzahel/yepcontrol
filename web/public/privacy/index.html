YepControl

Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

YepControl is operated by Graehl Arts (Kyle Graehl). It lets you pair a browser with an Android phone you control, then request access to that phone. No account is required. We do not include advertising, advertising identifiers, marketing analytics or an analytics SDK.

Your screen and controls

Screen images, video, text, control actions, device details and any PIN you enter remotely are encrypted between your phone and paired browser. Our relay forwards encrypted records when a direct connection is unavailable. The relay does not have the session keys needed to decrypt those records, and does not save screen recordings or control content.

The phone and your paired browser can read the content you deliberately share. Only pair browsers and devices you trust. Android can restrict protected screens and sensitive fields. Screen sharing and control require the relevant Android permissions and your approval.

Connection information we handle

Our relay stores random pairing identifiers, public cryptographic keys, signed pairing receipts, access settings and revocation records so it can authenticate paired devices and enforce your choices. These are connection metadata, not the contents of your screen. Browser nicknames, phone names, local activity history, private identity keys and saved PINs are not uploaded as readable relay data.

Like other network services, the relay receives your IP address and can observe connection times and encrypted traffic sizes. We use IP addresses for short-lived request-limit counters. We do not run a website analytics beacon or configure routine web-access logging for YepControl. Operational error logs may contain technical network information; we do not intentionally log pairing secrets or screen/control payloads.

Optional background connections

Cloudflare operates our hosted website and relay, including storage of the connection metadata described above. Cloudflare also processes network information to deliver and protect its infrastructure under its privacy policy.

If you enable background connection requests, the app uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations. An installation identifier is registered with our relay so it can ask Google to wake this app when an authorized browser requests a connection. A push contains connection-request identifiers and expiry information, never your screen, typed text, PIN or a command to control the phone.

Google processes installation and service metadata needed to operate Firebase. Direct WebRTC connections also use Google's public STUN service to discover network routes; that service can see network addresses, not the encrypted remote session. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy. We do not enable Google Analytics or Firebase message-delivery export to BigQuery.

Information kept on your devices

The Android app keeps paired-device settings, private identity keys and activity history locally. Activity records describe connection and settings events, not screenshots or typed content, and are limited to 30 days or 10,000 entries. An optional saved PIN is encrypted on the phone, excluded from Android backup and removable in the app. It is not sent to the relay or your browser as a saved credential.

Your browser uses local storage and IndexedDB for appearance preferences, pairing keys, saved phones and contact history. These are functional storage, not advertising or tracking cookies. Removing a phone from a browser deletes that browser's saved entry; revoke the browser in the phone app to remove its permission to connect.

Retention and your choices

When Allow unrestricted remote control is on, trusted paired controllers can operate this app’s configuration, change unlock settings and add independently removable controllers. For Android 0.10.0 only, an upgrade with an existing non-revoked pairing enables this setting once. New installations default to off. You can change the setting in the app’s Settings; paused or removed access and per-device unlock permission remain separate.

You can turn off remote access, disable background requests, remove a paired device and forget a saved PIN in the app. Changes that need to reach our relay take effect there when the phone can connect. Removing a paired device revokes its access and removes its background-wake address from the active registry.

We retain revoked pairing identifiers, public keys and receipts to prevent reuse of old grants. Relay-storage recovery history and retained encrypted authority backups can contain pairing metadata, revocation records and background-wake addresses. They do not contain screen content, private identity keys or PINs. Cloudflare provides a rolling relay-storage recovery history for up to 30 days; an address removed from active storage can remain in that history or in retained backups. We do not currently apply a fixed expiration to revocation records or authority backups. Google controls retention of data within Firebase under its own policies.

Request data deletion

To request deletion of identifiable connection metadata, email graehlarts@gmail.com with the subject “YepControl data deletion”. Describe the device or pairing concerned. We may ask for a pairing or installation identifier and proof that you control the device. Do not email PINs, pairing secrets, private keys or screenshots containing private information. There is no YepControl account to delete.

Removing a paired device in the app deletes its active background-wake address from our relay when the phone can connect. You can also request that we remove an identifiable active wake address. Revoked pairing identifiers, public keys and receipts remain in revocation records and encrypted authority backups to prevent old access grants from being reused; these records currently have no fixed expiry. Removed wake addresses may remain in recovery history or retained backups as described above. Screen recordings, control content and private keys are not stored by our relay. Google manages data within Firebase under its own retention policies.

Sharing and international processing

We do not sell data or share it for advertising. Connection metadata is processed only to provide, secure and operate the service, including through infrastructure providers and Google where described above. Service providers may process data in countries other than yours. We may disclose information when legally required.

Children and policy changes

YepControl is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied identifiable information to the service. We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above.
