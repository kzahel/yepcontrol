Which device does what?

  1. The controlling device uses a browser

    Choose Control a phone to show a pairing code. This can be a computer, tablet or another phone.

  2. The phone being controlled uses the app

    Open YepControl on that phone and scan the code shown in the controlling browser.

  3. Approve on the phone

    Tap Allow in the app. The phone will appear in the controlling browser, ready to connect.

A browser is all you need

Reach your phone from a computer, tablet or another phone. No desktop app to install.

Encrypted between your devices

Screen content, controls and typed text are end-to-end encrypted between your phone and approved browser.

You decide who connects

Approve each browser on the phone. Pause access or remove a paired browser whenever you want.

Screen access needs your permission on the phone. Smooth video uses Android’s screen-sharing consent. Protected screens may be unavailable, and Android can delay background connections. After a restart, unlock the phone locally first.
